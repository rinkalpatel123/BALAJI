Sanyasi Jal

"Jal hi Jeevan Hai". Often one would have heard this quote. This is not merely a quote but its reality. Yet access to clean drinking water in India is a challenge for most people. In order to address the issue of clean drinking water, Bageshwar Dham has now taken up the responsibility of being a key figure in bridging this gap by soon launching “Sanyasi Jal”.

Bageshwar Dham is setting up a water bottling unit in Gada Village. This would be a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility and is powered by high-class, fully automated machinery with a production capacity of **3600 bottles per hour (BPH)**. This ensures not only efficiency and consistency but also maintains strict hygiene standards throughout the bottling process — allowing the delivery of premium mineral water at scale without compromising on quality.

Per Hour Capacity 3600 Bottles
Per Day Capacity 57,600 Bottles
Monthly Capacity 17,28,000 Bottles
Annual Capacity 2,07,36,000 Bottles

Features

The focus is on increased hydration levels. These bottles will be enriched with essential minerals and infused with 300% more oxygen to revitalize your body and mind.

Crafted for purity and performance, every sip supports better metabolism, enhanced energy levels, and improved overall wellness. The water will aim at not just to quench your thirst — but to fuel your life.

No compromise will be made on the quality of the water. For this reason the manufacturing plant will be equipped with an in-house microbiology and chemical laboratory for daily testing and quality control. Every batch of water will undergo rigorous microbiological and mineral analysis to ensure it meets the highest standards of purity, safety, and consistency.

The RO (Reverse Osmosis) plant is being built entirely with SS304 food-grade stainless steel, ensuring the highest levels of hygiene, durability, and corrosion resistance. This robust infrastructure guarantees that every drop of water is purified in a clean, safe, and contamination-free environment.

An advanced filling machine features a fully automatic rinsing, filling, and capping system, ensuring seamless and hygienic bottle processing from start to finish. Designed for precision and efficiency, this integrated system will also minimizes human contact, enhance productivity, and maintains the highest standards of cleanliness and quality in every bottle that will be produced.

Availability

With the plant currently being built and the pending regulatory approvals, Bageshwar Dham is expecting Sanyasi Jal to be available for the public by October 2025.

At the beginning, the water will be distributed mainly in the region surrounding Gada Village and will cater 100% to the water demand in Bageshwar Dham itself.

Gradually, Sanyasi Jal will be made available all over the country.
