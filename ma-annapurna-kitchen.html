Guruji started the Ma Annapurna Kitchen at Shri Bageshwar Dham in the year 2020. Through this Ma Annapurna Kitchen meals are provided to the devotees twice a day, both in the morning and evening absolutely free of cost. No questions are raised with regards to religion or caste or color or economic background, as any one who desire to seek food is welcomed to the Ma Annapurna Kitchen.

Over the years, thousands of people have been receiving free meals here daily. Guruji believes that there is no greater donation than food donation, and there is no greater act of compassion than feeding the hungry. In line with this principle, Bageshwar Dham Jan Seva Samiti Gadha is consistently operating this Ma Annapurna Kitchen.

More than 10,000 meals are served on a daily basis. State of the art machinery to standardize the quality of the food have also been installed.

Guruji now aims at being able to serve food to more people by not only growing the current capacity at Bageshwar Dham but also starting Ma Annapurna Kitchen at other locations, first of which will be at the Shri Bageshwardham Sanatan Math in Mumbai.
