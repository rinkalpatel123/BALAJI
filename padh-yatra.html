Whenever there is an attack on religion on this earth, God himself descends on earth in an incarnated form to re-establish order and takes away the burden of the earth. In Treta, God took incarnation as Shri Ram and in Dwapar as Shri Krishna.

A few years ago, Lord Shri Adi Shankaracharya ji did the Digvijay Yatra in order to re-establish Sanatan Dharma on this earth and to unify India. He not only made scholars of other sects his disciples but also united those who were divided in the name of different traditions in Sanatan Dharma. He established four monasteries in the four directions of India. Today again, Bharat Mata, the land of penance of Brahma-devoted sages and saints, is demanding the re-establishment of Sanatan Dharma in the same way. The task of doing this has been taken up by the Guruji.

Operation Sindoor proved that if you tease us, we will not let you go. Similarly, Gurujis Operation Hindutva Jagran says that we will not rest until we make India a Hindu nation. To achieve this, we will go from house to house on foot and awaken the sleeping Hindus. Till every Hindu wakes up, we will neither live in peace nor let others live in peace.

There may be many sects,

But we all Hindus are one.

Shri Shastri Ji has taken a divine and unique resolution to undertake padh yatra (foot march) across India with a social message- to eradicate discrimination based on caste, sect, forward-backward, untouchable-touchable, to form a strong Hindu society and to awaken the Hindus.

First 'Sanatan Ekta Padhyatra' 2024

First 'Sanatan Ekta Padhyatra' 2024 The first padhyatra was held in 2024 from 21 to 29 November from
Sri Bageshwar Dham to Sri Orchha Dham and spanned more than 180 Km.

During this walk many saints, sages, mahants, mahatmas, leaders, actors, businessmen, sportspersons, artists as well as the general public participated enthusiastically not only from India but also from other countries.

On an average more than 200,000 people joined this Padhyatra on a daily basis and demonstrated unity by shouting slogans and waving the Sanatan colored flag.

Sanatan Ekta Padyatra

Experience of the first 'Sanatan Ekta Padyatra' 2024

In our history, Foreign invaders had converted many of our innocent people by luring them with greed. In order to bring these people back, Guruji has undertaken this unique public awakening campaign called 'Sanatan Hindu Ekta'. Through this campaign Guruji is gathering lakhs of people by making them take a pledge and oath towards 'Sanatan Hindu Unity'. This movement has today become a revolution in the whole of India as well as the whole world. Hindus have started awakening.
