FAQS

What is a Darbar?

The Darbar of Bageshwar Dham is a chance to meet the revered Gurudev, take advice and guidance from him.

How Much does it cost to meet Guruji?

Meeting Guruji is absolutely free. Please do not fall into the trap of paying anyone money under the promise of meeting Guruji.

How to file Arzi in Bageshwar Dham ?

The process of Arzi for Bageshwar Dham is very simple. When you come to the Dham, you have to tie a coconut in a red cloth and keep it in the Dham premises. However, here you will see a coconut tied in red, yellow, and black cloth. The reason behind this is that if your Arzi is a general one, then tie the coconut in a red cloth, if the Arzi is related to a marriage, then tie the coconut in a yellow cloth and if the petition is related to a ghost, then tie the coconut in a black cloth. Many times, in his katha, revered Gurudev says that if you cannot do this by coming to the Dham, then you can do this at the place of worship in your home. Bageshwar Balaji Maharaj will listen to your request.

How many times is it necessary to appear?

Friends, when your Arzi is filed in the darbar, then in the divine court the revered Gurudev himself tells how much appearance you must make. Actually, every devotee is ordered to appear for at least five Tuesdays. You devotees can do more than this and make sure until your appearance is completed, consumption of alcohol, meat, garlic, and onion has to be completely prohibited.

Where is Shri Bageshwar Dham Located and how can you reach there?

Shri Bageshwar Dham is located in Chhatarpur District in the village of Gada. About 30 Km of distance from Khajuraho, which is also the closest airport. From Khajuraho Airport, the Dham is a 20 minute drive. The second option is Gwalior airport, which is a 3 hours drive from the Dham. For Railway travellers the two nearby stations are Khajuraho Or Chhatarpur station. For road travel one will have take the Jhansi - Khajuraho Four-lane ( National Highway 39) to reach the Dham.

What is the Glory of Bageshwar Dham?

Bageshwar Dham situated in Chhatarpur district is famous all over the world today. This temple is known for the self-manifested Balaji Hanuman and Bageshwar Mahadev who appeared here. It has a golden history as a temple of Bageshwar Mahadev of the Chandel period, which is about a thousand years old. Due to the fame of Pujya Pt. Shri Dhirendra Krishna Shastri ji, people from all over the world reach this temple for darshan. Lord Balaji seated here is considered to be self-manifested. It is believed that Pujya Dhirendra Krishna Shastri ji's great grandfather Pujya Pad Sanyasi Baba had performed hard sadhana here, after which his grandfather Shri Setulal Garg ji also made this place divine with his penance. Today the glory of this place is famous all over the world. It is believed that all kinds of auspicious resolutions are fulfilled by tying a coconut of prayer in front of Shri Balaji Hanuman seated here.

How can you meet Guruji ?

The divine great man Pt. Shri Dhirendra Krishna Shastri, the Peethadheeshwar of Shri Bageshwar Dham Siddha Peeth, works day and night to propagate Sanatan rituals in India and the entire world. He spends about 18 to 20 hours a day among the common people. His main residence is his hut built at Bageshwar Dham. In between, he propagates Sanatan through divine stories in the entire India and the world. You can get information about Gurudev's presence by contacting Bageshwar Dham's helpline numbers 7055005553 and 7055005554. When he is at the Dham, he personally meets more than a thousand people every day and holds a divine court in front of thousands of people. He can also be met in many cities of the country and abroad during the stories.

What are the special festivals of Shri Bageshwar Dham?

Four big festivals are also organized in a year at Shri Bageshwar Dham. The biggest festival is organized on the occasion of Mahashivratri. Under the guidance of Pujya Gurudev, on the occasion of Shivratri, the Bageshwar Dham Jan Seva Samiti organizes the marriage festival of girls from hundreds of poor and helpless families. On this occasion, saint sermons, storytelling and cultural programs are also organized. Similarly, the appearance festival of Shri Hanuman Ji Maharaj is also celebrated with great pomp at Shri Bageshwar Dham. Every year on this occasion, the people are connected to the Sanatan culture by organizing the stories of learned storytellers of the country. In the same sequence, a grand Guru Purnima festival is also organized at Shri Bageshwar Dham every year in which Guru Diksha, Sant Vani, cultural presentations are organized.

What is Urja Sanchay Samagam?

Guruji has innovated and shown the whole world the path of energy conservation sadhna which is organized through Urja Sanchay Samagam camps. Guruji says that today people in the country and the world are discussing body detox or body purification but discussion of purification of mind and intellect is more important than purification of the body. Urja Sanchay Samagam paves the way for purification of mind and intellect as well as connects man with the worship of Hanuman ji. Hundreds of people took advantage of a similar camp organized at Bageshwar Dham recently. Similarly, a camp was also organized at Parmarth Niketan Ashram in Rishikesh on the banks of the Ganga and Biruhali Jungle Resort in Katni district. In this camp, the Yajman are pre-registered and have to pay for their residential and food arrangements. After this, such determined Yajamans get to know about important activities from Pujya Gurudev like worship of Lord Surya in the morning, meditation of their deity by concentrating on breathing, worship of God by Yagya Havan. This camp is becoming a medium of accumulating energy for many people.

What is a Parcha?

With the grace of Shri Bageshwar Balaji and the blessings of Pujya Sanyasi Baba and Dada Guru, Pujya Gurudev Bhagwan has a Siddhi service. With the blessings of his Guru Sanyasi Baba, he writes the past, present and future of the person in front of him on a slip of paper. This is a matter of wonder and curiosity, to know, understand and receive this blessing, people from all over the world come to Bageshwar Dham. This slip is made on the orders of Shri Sanyasi Baba, the devotees sit in the court after chanting Ramnaam here, Pujya Gurudev calls such devotees to the court by name and then writes their life on paper. He writes the life of the devotees on paper.
