location

Shri Bageshwar Dham, located in village Gadha of Chhatarpur district of Madhya Pradesh. Gadha is a Village in Bijawar Tehsil in Chhatarpur District of Madhya Pradesh State, India. It belongs to Sagar Division . It is located 41 KM towards South from District headquarters Chhatarpur. 305 KM from State capital Bhopal.

Gadha is surrounded by Bada Malehara Tehsil towards west , Chhatarpur Tehsil towards North , Baldeogarh Tehsil towards west , Rajnagar Tehsil towards East . Chhatarpur , Nowgong , Panna , Tikamgarh are the near by Cities to Gadha.

Religious

Religious Significance

Shri Bageshwar Dham is a religious place known for the divinity of Swayambhu Hanuman Balaji. It is a sacred place where Shri Hanuman Balaji is worshipped in the form of Pran Prathishtit Vigrah (statue). Shri Bageshwar Dham is the divine land of many ascetics, where people get the blessings of Shri Hanuman Balaji just by doing his darshan. The temple is said to be more than 300 years old being built in the beginning of the 18th century.

At Shri Bageshwar Dham, thousands of devotees gather every day, to seek the blessings of Shri Hanuman Balaji thereby creating a festive atmosphere.

It is believed that at Shri Bageshwar Dham, Shri Hanuman Balaji listens to the problems of his devotees through “Arzi” and gives the solution through the Peethadhishwar of the Dham, Pujya Dhirendra Krishna Shastri Ji Maharaj, whom the world knows as Shri Bageshwar Dham Sarkar.

Footprint

Global Impact & Humanitarian Footprint

From India’s heartland to the world stage, Bageshwar Dham is committed to advancing human dignity, spiritual awakening, and sustainable development. Aligned with globally accepted humanitarian goals such as the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), our projects resonate beyond religious boundaries.

  • International Events & Tours: Guruji’s kathas and spiritual discourses have been hosted in the UK, US, UAE, and Europe, drawing global audiences who seek peace, healing, and transformation.
  • Volunteer Networks: A growing global network of volunteers and seva groups coordinate local charity events, language translations, food drives, and educational workshops.
  • Partnerships for Progress: Collaborating with like-minded NGOs, medical missions, and social enterprises for causes including healthcare access, education for underprivileged children, tribal upliftment, and gender equality.
  • Recognitions & Milestones: From felicitations in the UK Parliament to meetings with global leaders, Guruji’s mission is acknowledged as one that bridges tradition with modern humanitarian values.
  • Join the Movement: Whether you’re a spiritual seeker, donor, CSR partner, or volunteer—Bageshwar Dham offers you a platform to make a real difference. Together, we serve humanity
