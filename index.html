Dham's work

Discourse and Satsang

The preaching of the Katha from the mouth of Param Pujya Guruji is to propagate the Indian Sanatan tradition throughout the world and unite all people globally while bring a spiritual revolution in India. He aims to spread the message of love, peace and harmony in the world and to get people to commit towards promoting unity in the times of diversity.

Divya Darbar

In our ancient Vedic tradition, according to astrology, our ancient sages showed people guidance about the upcoming problems and pending problems and showed them the way out of darkness through the doors of knowledge. With the blessings of Hanumanji's divine powers, people entangled in the physical and divine problems of life are given solutions through the Divya Darbar of Guruji.

Negative Energy

It is believed that negative influences can cause various problems in people's lives. Guruji holds a Darbar which is dedicated solely to address and remove negative energy by identifying its nature and then providing remedies and spiritual guidance.
