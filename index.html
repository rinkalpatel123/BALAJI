On 23rd February 2025, the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra Modi, graced Bageshwar Dham with his esteemed presence to lay the foundation stone for a new Cancer Hospital. This significant event marked a major step forward in the region’s healthcare development, aiming to provide advanced medical facilities and specialized cancer treatment to patients in and around the area. The Prime Minister’s visit not only highlighted the government’s commitment to improving healthcare infrastructure but also inspired hope among the local community for accessible and quality medical care in the near future.
