Bageshwar Dham Sanatan Math

The first Dham located outside of Madhya Pradesh was opened in Bhiwandi (Mumbai) in April 2025. The mandir is located in Thane region of Maharashtra. Guruji aimed at developing this Dham in order to provide devotees a place of worship and connect with Bageshwar Balaji even if travelling to Madhya Pradesh was difficult for them.

Officially called BAGESHWAR DHAM SANATAN MATH, it is built on a total land area of 4.5 Acres with a total carpet area of Mandir being 20,000 sq ft.

The significance of the Mandir is that there are 4 Garbh Graha in the Mandir:

  • Ganesh ji
  • Bageshwar Balaji (Hanuman Ji)
  • Spatikeshwar Mahadev (Shiv Ji)
  • Ram Parivar

The Praan Prathishta for the Mandir was done over 10 days by Guruji himself, wherein an Akhand Jyot was brought from the Bageshwar Dham Temple in Madhya Pradesh

A Yagya shala has also been made measuring 41x41 feet in size. ⁠

Mandir is of G+1 structure with the ⁠ground floor having a Darbar Hall sprawling over 10,000 sq ft while the ⁠⁠first floor has 10000 sq ft of mandir area.
