  2. vision

  • To establish and run the organization for the promotion and protection of the eternal ancient concepts of universal good conduct, and righteous way of life as enshrined in the Vedas.
  • To promote Vedic education and awareness aligned with the principles of ancient scriptures through schools, ashrams, and community centres.
  • To promote and preserve ancient Indian knowledge systems, including Vedic and Sanskrit literature, and their application in modern life.
  • To establish and operate Gurukulams / Learning & Experience Centres as centres of holistic education, incorporating traditional wisdom with contemporary learning.

To establish, maintain, and facilitate places of meditation for spiritual growth, along with organizing public assemblies featuring preachers and spiritual leaders to promote Vedic Phylosophy and ancient Indian wisdom.

  • To promote and preserve traditional Indian literature and cultural heritage by facilitating education, research, and interpretation of ancient texts and philosophies.
  • To publish, distribute, and translate texts of Indian origin into various languages for the benefit of humanity.
  • To organize seminars, workshops, and conferences (both online and offline) to encourage dialogue on ancient Indian knowledge systems.

  • To provide scholarships, freeships, and financial assistance for education to underprivileged individuals, with a special focus on girl child and women, irrespective of caste, creed, or religion.
  • To establish and operate hostels for women and girls with minimal or no cost to ensure safety and dignity.

  • To promote sustainable livelihood opportunities for tribal communities, widows, farmers, and marginalized groups through skill development, entrepreneurship, and eco-friendly self-employment initiatives.
  • To support startups and social enterprises in remote areas with special emphasis on women entrepreneurs.

  • To promote global peace and harmony through interfaith dialogue, cultural exchange programs, and campaigns for non-violent conflict resolution.
  • To organize workshops and initiatives fostering tolerance, mutual respect, and coexistence among diverse communities.

  • To cultivate humanity and universal brotherhood by fostering values of mutual respect, empathy, and coexistence.
  • To develop initiatives that use spirituality as a tool for peace, eliminating animosity and promoting non-violent conflict resolution across communities.
  • To organize forums, discussions, and cultural exchange programs on peacebuilding through spiritual harmony.

  • To establish centres and programs dedicated to mental health awareness, counselling, and therapeutic services, addressing emotional resilience, trauma recovery, and holistic wellbeing for individuals and communities.
  • To implement programs addressing stress, anxiety, and depression, promoting emotional resilience and mental well-being.
  • To advocate for a "happy and holistic way of life", fostering mental clarity, balance, and inner peace through structured guidance.

To provide care, shelter, and skill development programs for the homeless and economically disadvantaged, helping them reintegrate into society with dignity.

  • To establish rehabilitation centres for addiction treatment, offering medical, psychological, and social support.
  • To implement life correction programs focusing on personal growth, vocational skills, and moral development for recovering individuals.

  • To establish and support centres promoting natural healing methods such as yoga, Ayurveda, Naturopathy, and traditional therapies.
  • To conduct workshops and wellness retreats to propagate a holistic and healthy lifestyle based on Indian systems of healing.

  • To create awareness and drive campaigns for environmental sustainability, tree plantation, and eco-restoration projects.
  • To promote the use of eco-friendly technologies and practices for environmental preservation and climate change mitigation.

  • To offer timely aid and relief during natural disasters, pandemics, or crises by distributing food, medical supplies, and basic necessities.
  • To collaborate with governmental and non-governmental bodies for long-term rehabilitation and community rebuilding efforts.

  • To inspire entrepreneurs and businesses to embrace ethical practices, spiritual values, and social responsibility in commerce.
  • To encourage philanthropy, social entrepreneurship, and giving back to society as a core business philosophy.

  • To partner with international organizations, academic institutions, and spiritual leaders to promote Vedic values globally.
  • To organize international summits, cultural programs, and collaborations for mutual learning and global harmony.

  • To promote gender equality and empower women through education, skill training, financial literacy, and leadership development.
  • To provide a safe and supportive environment for women and girls to thrive spiritually, emotionally, and professionally.

  • To preserve and promote traditional Indian arts, music, and dance forms through performances, workshops, and festivals.
  • To support artists and provide platforms to showcase cultural expressions rooted in Indian heritage.

  • To facilitate scientific research in Vedic disciplines such as cosmology, mathematics, health sciences, and consciousness studies.
  • To integrate ancient wisdom with modern research methodologies for global understanding and well-being.
