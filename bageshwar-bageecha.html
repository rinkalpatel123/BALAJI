Protection of the environment

*The resolutions of Guruji, have created a different definition of social welfare and public welfare. Devotees from India and abroad are joining these noble resolutions. You can also join the resolutions of respected Guruji by clicking on the below:
Donate

Protection of the environment has now become a global debate, with various governments all over the world realizing the negative effect of urbanization on the environment and subsequently on our future generations.

So far more than 500,000 trees have been planted and continue to increase.

The conservation of trees is essential for oxygen to remain in the environment. Keeping in mind our future generations Guruji has decided to contribute to the cause by taking a pledge to to protect the environment by building Bageshwar Bageechas around the country.
