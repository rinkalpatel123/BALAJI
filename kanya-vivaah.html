kanya Vivaah Timeline

2025 KANYA VIVAH
251 GiRLS MARRIED

This grand wedding ceremony was held on 26 February 2025, in which 251 girls were married. Out of these, 108 girls were from the tribal community and the remaining 143 were from other weaker sections. The economic condition of the families was checked through a well-organized selection process, so that the assistance reached only those who really needed it.

2024 KANYA VIVAH
151 GIRLS MARRIED

The number of marriages rose to 151 in the year. Each bride was gifted with items necessary for household life such as furniture, electronic gadgets and even motorcycles.

2023 KANYA VIVAH
121 girls married

The marriage ceremony was further expanded in which 125 girls got married. The Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh Shri Shivraj Singh Chauhan graced the event with his presence.

2022 KANYA VIVAH
108 GIRLS MARRIED

In the year 2022, the marriage of 108 girls was completed with the help of devotees and donors. This initiative is a reflection of the concept of Bageshwar Dham which is dedicated to the upliftment and spiritual awakening of poor families.

2021 KANYA VIVAH
51 girls married

In the year 2021, with the cooperation of Naugaon community and devotees, a dignified mass marriage of 51 girls was organized.

2020 KANYA VIVAH
5 GIRLS MARRIED

In the year 2020, the marriage of 5 girls was organized on Shri Ramlila Manas stage by Naugaon Agrawal Samaj and social worker Kumari Tripti Kathail and her team.

2019 KANYA VIVAH
17 girls married

In the year 2021, with the cooperation of Naugaon community and devotees, a dignified mass marriage of 51 girls was organized.
