Kanya Vivaah

This is the first and only pilgrimage in India where, every year on the occasion of Shivaratri, marriages of underprivileged or orphaned girls from all sections are arranged through donations received in the donation box. Since 2019, Bageshwar Dham has been conducting weddings for destitute and helpless girls from needy families. With the number of girls growing every year, in the year 2025 Bageshwar Dham organized the wedding of 251 Girls out of which 108 girls belonged to tribal families. The target is to reach 1008 girls on a yearly basis. During these celebrations, Guruji takes on the role of a father, taking on all the responsibilities involved in marrying a daughter. The couples are showered with plenty of gifts which vary every year and contain household items, jewellery, motor cycles, refrigerators etc. Guruji, himself goes around the world giving invitations to people to attend this event. The Kanya Vivah is attended by devotees from over the world including politicians, businessmen and celebrities. Till date Bageshwar Dham has facilitated the marriage of 1100 underprivileged girls from all over the country.

Timeline

kanya Vivaah Timeline

  • 2025 KANYA VIVAH

    251 GIRLS MARRIED

    This grand wedding ceremony was held on 26 February 2025, in which 251 girls were married. Out of these, 108 girls were from the tribal community and the remaining 143 were from other weaker sections. The economic condition of the families was checked through a well-organized selection process, so that the assistance reached only those who really needed it.

  • 2024 KANYA VIVAH

    151 GIRLS MARRIED

    The number of marriages rose to 151 in the year. Each bride was gifted with items necessary for household life such as furniture, electronic gadgets and even motorcycles.

  • 2023 KANYA VIVAH

    121 girls married

    The marriage ceremony was further expanded in which 125 girls got married. The Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh Shri Shivraj Singh Chauhan graced the event with his presence.

  • 2022 KANYA VIVAH

    108 GIRLS MARRIED

    In the year 2022, the marriage of 108 girls was completed with the help of devotees and donors. This initiative is a reflection of the concept of Bageshwar Dham which is dedicated to the upliftment and spiritual awakening of poor families.

  • 2021 KANYA VIVAH

    51 GIRLS MARRIED

    In the year 2021, with the cooperation of Naugaon community and devotees, a dignified mass marriage of 51 girls was organized.

  • 2020 KANYA VIVAH

    5 GIRLS MARRIED

    In the year 2020, the marriage of 5 girls was organized on Shri Ramlila Manas stage by Naugaon Agrawal Samaj and social worker Kumari Tripti Kathail and her team.

  • 2019 KANYA VIVAH

    17 GIRLS MARRIED

    In the year 2021, with the cooperation of Naugaon community and devotees, a dignified mass marriage of 51 girls was organized.
