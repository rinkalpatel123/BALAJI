Gowseva

*The resolutions of Guruji, have created a different definition of social welfare and public welfare. Devotees from India and abroad are joining these noble resolutions. You can also join the resolutions of respected Guruji by clicking on the below:
Donate

Guruji has initiated a nationwide campaign, inspiring the entire country and the Sanatan community, for the service of cows (Gowseva). Guruji states that creating cow shelters is not the solution; rather, every Hindu should adopt one cow. This way, we can free the mother cow from issues she faces. Embracing this resolution, a cow shelter has been established at Bageshwar Dham. Here, along with the unique Punganur breed of Andhra Pradesh, the rearing and nurturing of indigenous Bundelkhandi cows are taking place. Guruji regularly participates in cow service and also motivates people to be inspired by this cow service.
